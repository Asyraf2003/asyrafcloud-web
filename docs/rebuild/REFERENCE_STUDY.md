## Media contract

All photographic, project, interface, portrait, and background media slots begin empty. Their borders, registration marks, transparency, aspect ratio, overlap, and perspective establish the composition. No artificial screenshot, mock UI, stock photo, or reference-site asset fills them. The original procedural sculpture is generated in code.

`dist/content.js` contains the four primary project slots and all secondary slots. Supply a local `/assets/` image or video and its alt text independently. The empty state remains valid.

## Scope and honesty

These are design studies and an original implementation, not a claim of an award or exact parity with either reference's production. Material quality, scale, pacing, and behavior must be judged in the rendered implementation. The primary source of the visual comparison is the supplied recordings, which were inspected as time-indexed sequences.
